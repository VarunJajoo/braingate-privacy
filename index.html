1. Introduction

BrainGate ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Varun Jajoo, is a digital wellbeing and mindful focus application. BrainGate helps users reclaim focus and curb mindless scrolling by introducing deliberate cognitive friction (such as trivia questions and mindful pauses) before opening user-selected distracting apps.

This Privacy Policy describes how BrainGate handles user data and discloses our strict commitment to privacy, data transparency, and compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Prominent Disclosure: Android AccessibilityService API

BrainGate utilizes the Android AccessibilityService API (BrainGateAccessibilityService) to provide its core functionality.

Why BrainGate Uses the AccessibilityService API:

BrainGate uses the AccessibilityService solely to detect when user-selected protected applications are brought to the foreground (by listening for TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED events). When a protected app is opened, BrainGate intercepts the launch to display the intentional "Cognitive Gate" screen before granting temporary access.

What BrainGate Does NOT Do:

  • BrainGate does NOT read, inspect, or log any text you type (no keylogging).
  • BrainGate does NOT access or monitor personal messages, chats, contacts, emails, or on-screen content (canRetrieveWindowContent is strictly set to false).
  • BrainGate does NOT record screen audio, video, or photos.
  • BrainGate does NOT alter or modify user settings without permission.
  • BrainGate does NOT transmit any accessibility data off your device. All detection and processing occurs in real-time, 100% locally on your device.

Enabling the Accessibility Service is completely optional and strictly controlled by the user. You can enable or disable this permission at any time from your device's system settings under Accessibility > Downloaded Apps > BrainGate.

3. Information Collected & Processed

A. Installed Applications Query

To allow you to choose which apps you wish to protect with BrainGate, the app queries your device's launchable applications list (using standard Android launcher intent resolution).

  • This package information is displayed exclusively inside BrainGate on the "Add Apps" screen.
  • Your list of installed applications is never transmitted, shared, or uploaded to any remote server or third party.

B. Focus Statistics & Streaks

BrainGate locally tracks your streaks, solved question count, and intentional unlock history to help visualize your progress. This data is saved strictly inside the app's local private sandbox (SQLite database and encrypted SharedPreferences).

C. Device Permissions

  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to automatically restore your protected app configuration and monitoring service after your phone restarts.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used only to deliver local notifications you request, such as notification when an app unlock duration has expired or daily mindfulness reminders.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Optional permission requested to prevent the operating system from prematurely stopping the app monitoring service in the background.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

BrainGate does not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties.

  • No Analytics SDKs: BrainGate does not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any third-party behavioral telemetry.
  • No Advertising Networks: The app is completely ad-free.
  • No Cloud Sync / Remote Servers: We operate zero backend servers for collecting user telemetry.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored strictly locally on your device:

  • You can reset or wipe all stored settings and statistics at any time through the in-app settings.
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling BrainGate from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes all data associated with the app from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

BrainGate does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is designed for general audiences seeking digital wellbeing tools and does not collect personal identifiers from any user.

7. Security

Your privacy is protected by the architecture of the app itself: by not transmitting any data over the internet, your personal data cannot be intercepted, leaked, or exposed on remote servers. All local preferences are protected by Android's application sandboxing model.
